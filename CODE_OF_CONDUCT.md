# Code of Conduct

Kant is built in the open by people who care about private communication. This
document sets the expectations for everyone who takes part: in issues, pull
requests, discussions, and on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/kdn2tAPtRX).

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Argue in good faith. Technical debate about the protocol, the ratchet or the
  relay is welcome; assume the other person is trying to get it right too.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect privacy. This is a privacy project: don't share anyone's personal
  information, messages, keys, or identifying details without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats, in public or in private.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background,
  or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Doxxing, or publishing private communications without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing threads.
- Encouraging any of the above.

## Security issues are not conduct issues

Please don't disclose vulnerabilities publicly. Report them privately as
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, contact a maintainer privately on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/kdn2tAPtRX), or use GitHub's "Report content"
option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. The response depends on how
serious the behaviour is and whether it is repeated.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.
