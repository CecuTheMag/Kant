# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report privately via GitHub:
https://github.com/CecuTheMag/Kant/security/advisories/new

Please include what's affected (app, core, relay, desktop, CLI), the version,
steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 7 days.
- A fix or mitigation plan within 90 days, and we'll agree the disclosure date with you.
- Credit in the advisory and release notes, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

Crypto and protocol (`packages/core`), the relay (`packages/relay`), and the
clients (`packages/app`, `packages/desktop`, `packages/cli`). The threat model is at
https://kant.network/security.
