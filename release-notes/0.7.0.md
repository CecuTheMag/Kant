# Kant 0.7.0 (Beta)

**Safer groups: nobody can post as someone else, history survives a key change, and leaving actually tells the group. Plus screen security on Android.**

## New

- **Screen security (Android).** Settings → Security → Screen security hides Kant in the recent-apps list and blocks screenshots and screen recording of the app.
- **Leaving a group tells the group.** The other members stop sending to you and the group's creator changes the group key, so you can't read anything sent after you left. Before, leaving only deleted the group on your own device.
- **A Content-Security-Policy in every build**, so no injected or remote script can run inside the app.
- **Desktop apps run on Electron 45.** Electron 33 no longer received security fixes.
- **Relays run on Node 22**, and have a separate operator token, `RELAY_ADMIN_TOKEN`, for `/admin/*` (and `/metrics` behind a tunnel).

## Fixed

- **Group messages could be sent under another member's name.** Kant now accepts a group message, edit, deletion, reaction or read receipt only when it arrives from the device of the member it names, and only from members of that group. Before, any member could post, edit or delete as someone else.
- **Anyone could re-point a group member's address.** A group "I'm online" notice is now accepted only from the member it names and only with an address that leads to them, as direct messages already required.
- **Files could be sent under a contact's name.** A file is accepted only from the device of the sender it names.
- **Group history turned into "[encrypted]" after the group's key changed**, on every device, and a message sent at that moment could be lost. Devices now keep the group's earlier keys for reading history, and accept a message sent with the previous key for ten minutes after a change.
- **Members who were offline never got the group key.** A group created or re-keyed while a member was offline now delivers their key when they come back, including a group created while you were offline yourself.
- **Read receipts in groups** never arrived; they do now.
- **Desktop notifications after a relay restart.** Kant registers for notifications again whenever it reconnects, not only when its address changes.

## Tested before release

Everything 0.6.0 was tested with, plus:

- an Android phone (Nothing Phone 1, Android 15) against the live relay with two desktop users: setup, adding contacts by link, messages both ways and in order, a group with messages from every member, leaving the group (the others see it, the one who left gets nothing more, and everyone's history stays readable), and screen security (screenshots come out black)
- two linked relays on Node 22, keeping their addresses and keys through the upgrade
- the Mac app on Electron 45: it starts, its built-in relay answers, and the UI renders
- the full test lab (a real relay, browsers driving the app's screens) and every protocol and app test

## After updating

- **Nothing changes for your chats**: 0.7.0 talks to 0.6.x apps and relays, and your chats, contacts and keys are untouched. Members still on 0.6.x aren't told when someone leaves a group and keep showing them as a member until they update.
- **Group history that already showed "[encrypted]"** after an earlier key change stays that way: older versions didn't keep the old key.
- **Relay operators**: rebuild and restart your relay to move it to Node 22 (re-run `scripts/deploy-relay.sh` or `scripts/relay-tunnel.sh`, or `docker compose ... up -d --build`). Its address and keys stay the same. Those scripts move an existing `RELAY_LAB_CONTROL_TOKEN` over to `RELAY_ADMIN_TOKEN`; don't set `RELAY_LAB_CONTROL_TOKEN` on a real relay, as it also turns on the test lab's restart endpoint.
