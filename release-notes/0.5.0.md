# Kant 0.5.0 (Beta)

**Big groups and busy contact lists work now, and every release is built and checked automatically.**

## Fixed

- **The Kant logo shows on the desktop welcome screen.** It was missing in the desktop app, because the app looked for it at the root of the disk.
- **You can talk to more than 32 people at once.** Every person you're connected to through a relay used up one slot on your connection to that relay, and there were only 32 slots. The 33rd person, whether a member of a big group or the 33rd contact online at the same time, couldn't be reached at all: Kant kept retrying and the connection was refused every time. Both the app and the relay now allow 1,024. We tested this with one person reaching 70 people at once, and with a 50-member group where everyone gets the group key and the first message.

- **"Online" now means online.** A contact who went offline used to stay marked "Online" until you restarted Kant, and someone you were actively chatting with could show "Last seen" instead. Kant now shows someone as online if it has heard from them in the last two and a half minutes, by a message or a presence ping.

## New

- **Windows.** Kant now comes as a Windows installer (`Kant-Setup-0.5.0-x64.exe`) and a portable version that needs no install (`Kant-0.5.0-x64-portable.exe`). Most PCs need the x64 files; the `-arm64` versions are for Windows on ARM (e.g. Snapdragon laptops). The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will say the publisher is unknown; choose "More info" → "Run anyway" after checking the file's SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- **Releases are built in the open.** The Android, Linux and Windows apps are now built by GitHub Actions from the tagged source code. Every build is checked before it's published: it has to start, its built-in relay has to answer, and the app has to load. The Android app has to be signed with the same key as before. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of every file.
- **Update notifications on Android without the Play Store.** Add `https://github.com/CecuTheMag/Kant` to [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) and turn on "Include prereleases".

## Tested before release

The browser test suite drives the real app through the same screens a person uses, against real relays. It had fallen behind the redesigned interface in 0.4.1 and was rebuilt for this release. It now runs on every change and covers:

- messages in order both ways, and no duplicates
- messages sent while the other person is offline
- a 1 MB file arriving byte for byte
- the relay restarting mid-conversation and mid-group-setup
- group members leaving and coming back
- groups of 10 and 50 people
- phones switched off and on in every order across two relays

## After updating

- **Relay operators: update your relay.** The fix for talking to more than 32 people needs both halves. Until your relay is updated, people using it are still limited to 32 at a time, even on 0.5.0. The new `RELAY_MAX_CIRCUITS_PER_PEER` setting (default 1024) controls the limit.
- Nothing else changes: 0.5.0 talks to 0.4.x apps and relays, and your chats, contacts and keys are untouched.
