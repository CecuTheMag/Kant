# Changelog

What changed in each release, written for people who use Kant, is in
[`release-notes/`](release-notes/) and on the
[releases page](https://github.com/CecuTheMag/Kant/releases):

- [0.6.0](release-notes/0.6.0.md) — Mac and iPhone; host a relay without port forwarding
- [0.5.0](release-notes/0.5.0.md) — automated, checksummed releases; Windows

Earlier releases (0.2.0 – 0.4.3) are described on the releases page only.

Clients and relays stay wire-compatible across minor versions; anything that
needs an operator or a user to act is listed under "After updating" in that
release's notes.
